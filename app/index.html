Patient Guidelines and OPD Facilities

Information regarding clinic check-in procedures, consultations, and triage.

Consultation Timings

Morning sessions operate from 09:00 AM to 01:00 PM. Evening sessions operate from 04:30 PM to 08:30 PM. Each consultation slot covers 15 to 30 minutes depending on clinical examination requirements.

Token Calling Procedure

Tokens are called in sequence. Please check in at the reception desk 10 minutes prior to your scheduled slot. If your consultation is delayed due to an in-room emergency, the receptionist will update the queue feed.

Walk-In Triage

Physical walk-in patients are registered directly at the front desk. Acute or urgent medical complaints undergo immediate clinical triage and are slotted into available openings.
