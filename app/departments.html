Clinical Divisions

Outpatient Departments and Diagnostic Units

SHIFA CLINIC houses dedicated outpatient divisions providing specialty consultations, clinical evaluations, and diagnostic procedures across six medical disciplines.

Room 101 & Room 106

General and Internal Medicine

Comprehensive care for primary illnesses, chronic adult disorders, and seasonal infections.

Clinical Scope: Type-2 Diabetes management, systemic hypertension, infectious fevers, respiratory conditions, geriatric medical screenings.
In-Clinic Procedures: Continuous blood pressure profiling, rapid blood sugar testing, nebulization therapy, adult immunizations.
Faculty: Dr. Mohammed Farhan (MBBS, MD) & Dr. Ananya Balaji (MBBS, MD).
09:00 AM to 08:30 PM
Room 104

Cardiology and Cardiovascular Care

Specialized diagnostic assessment and management of cardiac health and arterial wellness.

Clinical Scope: Angina evaluation, post-angioplasty reviews, arrhythmias, dyslipidemia, preventive cardiac risk profiling.
In-Clinic Procedures: 12-lead digital electrocardiography (ECG), ambulatory Holter monitoring, acute cardiac enzyme testing.
Faculty: Dr. Aravind Krishnan (MBBS, MD Cardiology, FACC).
Session 10:00 AM to 07:30 PM
Room 102

Pediatrics and Neonatology

Dedicated medical care for infants, children, and adolescents in an isolated child-friendly environment.

Clinical Scope: Neonatal developmental assessments, childhood allergies, gastrointestinal conditions, pediatric respiratory infections.
In-Clinic Procedures: Complete childhood vaccination according to national immunization schedules, percentile growth monitoring.
Faculty: Dr. Sneha Parthasarathy (MBBS, DCH, MD Pediatrics).
09:30 AM to 08:00 PM
Room 105

Orthopedics and Musculoskeletal Care

Diagnosis and non-operative management of joint diseases, bone traumas, and spine disorders.

Clinical Scope: Osteoarthritis of the knee and hip, lumbar back pain, cervical spondylosis, athletic sprains, osteoporosis.
In-Clinic Procedures: Minor splinting, joint aspiration, intra-articular therapeutic injections, fracture stabilization.
Faculty: Dr. Priya Ranganathan (MBBS, MS Orthopedics, MCh).
10:00 AM to 08:00 PM
Room 103

Dermatology and Skin Health

Evidence-based clinical care for dermatological, allergic, hair, and nail pathology.

Clinical Scope: Atopic dermatitis, chronic psoriasis, acne vulgaris, fungal and bacterial cutaneous infections, urticaria.
In-Clinic Procedures: Dermatoscopic evaluation of pigmented lesions, comedone extraction, cryotherapy for viral warts.
Faculty: Dr. Vikramaditya Sen (MBBS, MD, DVD Dermatology).
04:30 PM to 08:30 PM
Laboratory Suite B

Clinical Pathology and Diagnostics

Rapid on-site biochemical, hematological, and microbiological diagnostic support.

Available Tests: Complete Blood Count (CBC), Fasting & Postprandial Glucose, HbA1c, Lipid Profiles, Renal Function Tests, Liver Panels.
Rapid Screenings: Dengue NS1 antigen, Malaria smear, Urine routine examination, Serum Electrolytes.
Report Delivery: Routine blood profiles processed within 3 hours. Direct digital delivery to doctor consultation desks.
